## What this supports

- The stock `segment1` payload runs the **programmer/Sahara** path, not the
  storage-XBL path.
- `ctx+0xB8` is set (secure mode), and the XBL_SEC image is present at
  `0x14699000`.
- The live descriptor type is `0xA` with `[+0x20]=0x31194C`.

## What this does not support

- That the storage path cannot be reached with a different context.
- That the PBL enforces the XBL signature on the storage path.
- That a payload can load XBL from storage.

## Notes

- The raw capture is private; the probe binary is not published.
- The probe loops in PBL by design; a cold power cycle restores Android.
