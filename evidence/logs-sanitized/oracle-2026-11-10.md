## What this supports

- The payload at `segment1:0x970` ran after segment2/3.
- The PBL transmit helper was callable from the payload.
- The magic is device-generated, not echoed host data: it is sent after the
  last host write and contains a payload-specific string.

## What this does not support

- EL3 privilege escalation.
- Persistent modification.
- Bootloader unlock.
- Execution on a different firmware or SoC.

## Notes

- On the Windows serial path, the COM port is lost after segment3, so the
  magic is not observable there. The macOS libusb path survives the handoff
  with the two HELLO drains.
- The raw capture is private. The payload binary is not published.
